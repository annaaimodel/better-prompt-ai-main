# AURUM — Business Plan & Monetisation Strategy

*The gold standard for the high-ticket sales job search.*
Last updated: 16 Jun 2026

---

## 1. The one-line thesis
AURUM is the trusted home for **remote high-ticket sales careers** (closers,
setters, SDRs, customer success, VAs). We win the **job-seeker audience** with the
cleanest, most-vetted board in a noisy, scammy niche — then monetise that audience
three ways: **paid tools for seekers, placements for employers, and affiliate/
sponsorship income** on top.

The asset that makes the money is **the audience and the trust**, not the board
itself. The board is the magnet.

---

## 2. Why this can work (the wedge)
- **The niche is noisy and low-trust.** Most "high-ticket" listings are surveys,
  MLM funnels or course pitches. AURUM's vetting (remote-only, English, real roles,
  no surveys/scams) is a genuine differentiator. *Trust is the moat.*
- **The audience already congregates** — in the 89 Skool/Discord/Facebook/LinkedIn
  communities in our directory. We don't have to create demand; we aggregate it.
- **The money is already flowing** in this space: paid communities ($27–200/mo),
  recruiting agencies charging to place closers, and people happily paying for an
  edge. We're inserting into proven spending behaviour.
- **Costs are near-zero.** Vercel, GitHub Actions and Google Sheets are free; the
  AI tools cost pennies per use. Gross margin is ~99%. The constraint is **traffic
  and trust**, not money — so every pound of revenue is almost pure profit.

---

## 3. Who pays us (two sides)

### A. Job seekers (B2C — volume, low ticket)
Remote sales pros and aspirants who want roles *and* an edge in landing them.
- Pain: roles are scattered; applications are generic; they don't stand out.
- Willingness to pay: moderate, but high intent (a single placement = life-changing
  income for them, so the tools are cheap by comparison).

### B. Employers / agencies (B2B — fewer, high ticket)
Coaches, agencies, info-product businesses and SaaS companies hiring remote closers
and setters. **This is where the real money is.**
- Pain: finding *vetted* sales talent fast is hard and expensive.
- Willingness to pay: high — a good closer is worth tens of thousands in revenue, so
  a placement fee of £500–£2,000 is an easy yes.

## 5. The product ladder (free → paid)
1. **Free:** browse the board, CV match, communities directory. *(Acquisition + SEO.)*
2. **Email list (free):** role alerts — *"3 new Closer roles in the US that match you."*
   The list is the single most valuable asset; everything monetises off it.
3. **Premium (£12/mo or £97/yr):**
   - Instant + early access to new roles (before the free board)
   - Unlimited CV match
   - AI cover letters + Loom scripts (already built)
   - Application tracker
4. **Pay-per-tool (no subscription):** £9 per cover letter / £15 per Loom script /
   £29 "application pack" — for people who won't subscribe.
5. **Employer side:** featured listings and **done-for-you placements** (we source &
   vet candidates from our pool; they pay on hire).

## 6. Pricing & unit economics (illustrative)

**Costs are trivial.** Per AI generation (Sonnet): ~£0.01–0.03. Hosting: £0. Domain:
~£1/mo. So margins are ~99% and the model lives or dies on **traffic → email → paid**.

**Worked example at modest scale** (achievable in 3–6 months with consistent posting
in the communities + SEO):

- 5,000 monthly visitors → 10% join the email list = **~500 new subscribers/mo**
- Of engaged subscribers, 2–3% take premium → **50–150 paying** × £12 =
  **£600–£1,800/mo recurring**
- Pay-per-tool from non-subscribers: ~£200–£500/mo
- **Placements:** just **2–4 hires/mo** at £500–£1,000 = **£1,000–£4,000/mo**
- Affiliate + sponsorship: £100–£500/mo passive

**Blended target: £2k/mo within ~90 days, £5–10k/mo within 6–9 months** — driven
mostly by placements and premium. These are *targets, not promises*; the lever is
audience growth.

> Key insight: **one employer placement ≈ 40–80 premium subscribers in revenue.**
> Don't neglect the B2B side just because B2C feels easier.

## 7. The funnel (how it all connects)
```
Communities / SEO / Socials  →  Free Board (trust + value)  →  Email List
        →  Premium subscription  +  Pay-per-tool
        →  (data/insight)  →  Employer placements & featured listings
        →  Newsletter sponsorships + Affiliate (passive, scales with list)
```
Every new free user feeds the list; the list feeds B2C revenue *and* makes the B2B
side possible (you can't place candidates you don't have).

## 8. Go-to-market (free distribution first)
1. **Be where the demand already is:** post value in the 89 communities in our
   directory (where rules allow), plus r/sales, r/WorkOnline. Lead with usefulness,
   not ads. *(See `launch-kit.md` for ready copy.)*
2. **SEO compounding:** sitemap + share previews are live. Add a simple blog later
   ("Best remote closer jobs this week", "How to land a high-ticket setter role") to
   capture search traffic forever.
3. **Socials:** claim handles, post the share card + role highlights. Each new role is
   a free piece of content ("New: $8k/mo Closer role, remote 👇").
4. **Email is the engine:** every visitor offered the alert signup; weekly "best new
   roles" send keeps you in their inbox.

## 9. 90-day action plan

**Days 0–30 — Switch on B2C + start manual B2B**
- [ ] Rename URL + verify signups work (Apps Script redeployed).
- [ ] Turn the private AI tools (cover letter/Loom) into a **public paid feature**
      (Stripe payment link or simple checkout) — or a premium gate.
- [ ] Launch the **weekly email** of best new roles to grow + warm the list.
- [ ] **Manual placements:** DM 10–20 companies posting in the communities — *"I have
      vetted remote closers/setters, want intros?"* Charge on hire. (Proves B2B demand
      with zero product needed.)
- **Goal:** first £ in (a tool sale or a placement) + 250+ email subs.

**Days 31–60 — Productise what works**
- [ ] Add a simple **employer page**: "Hiring? Post a featured role / get vetted
      candidates." Charge for featured posts.
- [ ] Formalise premium (alerts + tools + early access) with a clear pricing page.
- [ ] First newsletter sponsor or affiliate deal (the communities pay for referrals).
- **Goal:** £1–2k/mo blended; 3+ employer relationships.

**Days 61–90 — Scale the winners**
- [ ] Double down on whichever stream is converting best (likely placements or premium).
- [ ] Launch a lightweight **AURUM community** (Skool/Discord) if there's pull —
      recurring revenue + stronger moat.
- [ ] Start the SEO blog for compounding free traffic.
- **Goal:** £2–5k/mo; a repeatable placement process.

## 10. KPIs to watch
- **Top of funnel:** monthly visitors, email signups, signup rate.
- **B2C:** free→premium conversion %, MRR, churn.
- **B2B:** employer leads, featured listings sold, **placements/mo**, avg fee.
- **Passive:** affiliate revenue, sponsor revenue.
- **North star:** **MRR + monthly placement revenue.**

## 11. Risks & how we beat them
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Seekers leave once hired (churn) | Lean on **employer revenue** (doesn't churn the same way) + alumni referrals + "come back for your next role". |
| Chicken-and-egg (jobs vs. users) | We already have **demand** via the communities; court employers second. |
| Niche looks scammy by association | **Vetting = the brand.** "No surveys, no funnels" is the positioning. Protect it ruthlessly. |
| Competitors (Nomadic Closer, Dial A Closer, RCC, Closers.io) | Win on **trust + curation + free tools + a real brand (AURUM)**, not raw listing count. |
| Solo founder / time | Automate sourcing (already done) so your time goes to **placements & partnerships** — the high-value work. |
| Platform/API risk | Cheap, swappable infra; keep the email list exportable (you own it). |

---

## 12. The honest bottom line
- The **fastest cash** is **manual placements** — no new product needed, just DMs to
  companies already hiring in the communities. Start there this week.
- The **most durable cash** is **premium subscriptions + a weekly email**, because it
  compounds and uses tools you've already built.
- The **biggest long-term prize** is becoming the **trusted brand + talent pool** for
  remote high-ticket sales — at which point employers pay to access *you*.

Build the audience. Guard the trust. Monetise both sides. Everything else is detail.
