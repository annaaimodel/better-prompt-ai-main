## 🎬 1. Intro reel: "On Monday, I start again" (45–60s)

**Setting:** the quiet house. Start on a shot of an empty kid's bedroom or the kitchen table with too many chairs, then cut to you on the sofa.

> **[B-roll: empty bedroom / quiet hallway, then you to camera]**
> This is what an empty nest sounds like. *(pause, let the silence sit)*
>
> **[To camera]**
> My kids have grown up and gone. I'm [4_], I'm fat, I'm fun, and if I'm honest I've lost myself a bit.
>
> So on Monday, I'm starting a 90-day reset of my whole life.
>
> **[Quick cuts, one per line, a prop for each]**
> My health: high protein, two litres of water. *(water bottle)*
> One workout, every single day. *(trainers on the floor)*
> My money: no credit card, more income, and a new closing offer. *(card into the freezer)*
> And my head: reading again, and getting better at my job every day. *(book + headphones)*
>
> **[To camera, softer]**
> I'm going to film every single day. The good bits, the bad bits, the real numbers.
>
> If you're in your 40s or 50s and wondering "what now?", come with me.
>
> Follow, and I'll see you Monday. Day 1. 💛

**On-screen text:** "My kids left home. On Monday I start rebuilding my life. 90 days. Every day filmed."
**Caption:** "Empty nest, full life 💛 90 days to get me back. Health, fitness, money, career and my mind. Day 1 is Monday. Who's coming with me? #emptynest #over40 #midlifereset #90daychallenge #startingover"
**Engagement ask:** pin a comment: "Tell me how old your youngest is and how you're feeling 👇"

## 🥗💪 2. Health & Fitness prep: "Getting ready for Monday" (40–60s)

**Setting:** kitchen, then living room / garden for the fitness test.

> **[Hook, holding up a family-size bag of something]**
> Twenty years of cooking for a family, and my cupboards still think there are [5] of us.
>
> **[Clearing out, quick cuts]**
> Monday I start 90 days of high protein, fewer carbs. So the [crisps / bread / pasta] are going to [a neighbour / the food bank]. *(into a box)*
>
> **[Restock]**
> In: [3 real examples, e.g. eggs, chicken, Greek yoghurt, protein shakes, big bag of salad].
>
> **[2L water bottle, marked with times]**
> Two litres of water a day. I've drawn the times on the bottle because I know myself. 😂
>
> **[Workout clothes laid out]**
> And one workout every single day. Any workout. A walk counts, a home video counts. It just has to happen.
>
> **[Cut to you doing the baseline test, quick clips]**
> This is my Day 0 fitness test: push-ups, sit-to-stands and a plank. I'm doing it again on Day 90. Brace yourselves. 😅
>
> **[To camera]**
> Are you starting with me? Tell me your one health goal 👇

**On-screen text:** "Day 0: prepping my body for a 90-day life reset"

## 💷💼 4. Money & Career prep: "My credit card is going in the freezer" (45–60s)

**Setting:** kitchen. A bowl of water, the freezer, then your desk.

> **[Hook, holding up your credit card]**
> For the next 90 days, this is going in the freezer. Literally.
>
> **[Freeze it: card into a tub of water, into the freezer]**
> No credit card for 90 days. I've taken it off Amazon, off my phone, off everything. If I can't pay for it with money I actually have, I don't need it.
>
> **[At your desk]**
> And the other half: earning more. At [4_] I changed career into high-ticket sales. That's talking to people on calls and earning commission when they buy.
>
> Where I am right now: [honest sentence, e.g. "I'm looking for my next closing offer"].
> My goal for these 90 days: **secure a new closing offer** and get my income up. I'll be applying and practising every day, and I'll show you the real numbers. Even the rubbish ones.
>
> **[To camera]**
> Who else is ditching the credit card with me? 🧊👇

**On-screen text:** "No credit card for 90 days + chasing a new closing offer at [4_]"
**Rules:** real numbers only. When you share earnings, add "not typical, results vary" in the caption.

## Weekend filming checklist
- [ ] **Light:** face a window; avoid overhead lights. A £20 ring light helps for evening diaries
- [ ] **Sound:** film close to the phone or use a £15 clip-on mic. Sound matters more than video quality
- [ ] **Batch it:** film all 5 on Saturday, edit in CapCut, and schedule them
- [ ] **Captions:** CapCut auto-captions, big and centred (most people watch on mute)
- [ ] **Cover image:** the same style for every video ("Day X/90" in the same font and colour) so your grid looks like a series
- [ ] **Day 0 private record:** photos, measurements, screen time, bank or income snapshot, energy/mood score. You'll use these on Day 90
- [ ] **Hold the guide link for now:** keep the "Comment AGAIN" CTA for Day 1 onwards (Week 1 of the guide needs writing first)
