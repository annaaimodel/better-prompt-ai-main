## 🎬 1. Intro reel: "On Monday, I start again" (45–60s)

**Setting:** the quiet house. Start on a shot of an empty kid's bedroom or the kitchen table with too many chairs, then cut to you on the sofa.

> **[B-roll: empty bedroom / quiet hallway, then you to camera]**
> This is what an empty nest sounds like. *(pause, let the silence sit)*
>
> **[To camera]**
> My kids have grown up and gone. I'm [4_], I'm fat, I'm fun, and if I'm honest I've lost myself a bit.
>
> So on Monday, I'm starting a 90-day reset of my whole life.
>
> **[Quick cuts, one per line, a prop for each]**
> My body. *(trainers on the floor)*
> My mind. *(stack of books)*
> My money: I've changed career into high-ticket sales. *(laptop)*
> And by the end of it, I want to be living abroad. *(map or passport)*
>
> **[To camera, softer]**
> I'm going to film every single day. The good bits, the bad bits, the real numbers.
>
> If you're in your 40s or 50s and wondering "what now?", come with me.
>
> Follow, and I'll see you Monday. Day 1. 💛

**On-screen text:** "My kids left home. On Monday I start rebuilding my life. 90 days. Every day filmed."
**Caption:** "Empty nest, full life 💛 90 days to get me back. Body, mind, money and a move abroad. Day 1 is Monday. Who's coming with me? #emptynest #over40 #midlifereset #90daychallenge #startingover"
**Engagement ask:** pin a comment: "Tell me how old your youngest is and how you're feeling 👇"

## 🥗 2. Body prep: "Getting my kitchen ready for Monday" (40–60s)

**Setting:** kitchen. Show the cupboards before and after, then the shopping.

> **[Hook, holding up a family-size bag of something]**
> Twenty years of cooking for a family, and my cupboards still think there are [5] of us.
>
> **[Clearing out, quick cuts]**
> So today I'm getting my body ready for Monday. First: the cupboard clear-out. *(bag of snacks into a donation box)* Not throwing it away, just not keeping it here.
>
> **[Shopping / fridge restock]**
> Restocked with stuff I actually want to eat: [3 real examples, e.g. eggs, berries, chicken, big bag of salad].
>
> **[Trainers by the door]**
> Trainers by the door. My only rule for Week 1: a 20-minute walk every day. That's it. No crazy diet.
>
> **[To camera]**
> And I've taken my Day 0 photos and measurements. I'm not showing you yet. Maybe on Day 90. 😅
>
> Are you starting with me? Tell me your one body goal 👇

**On-screen text:** "Prepping my body for a 90-day life reset (Day 0)"
**Pinned line:** "Sharing what I'm doing, not medical advice. Check with your GP before changing diet or exercise."

## 📚 3. Mind prep: "My phone is going in a drawer" (40–60s)

**Setting:** bedroom / cosy chair. Books, a notebook, your phone.

> **[Hook, showing phone screen time]**
> My screen time this week was [X hours]. That's [Y] hours a day of scrolling other people's lives instead of living mine.
>
> **[Quick cuts]**
> So here's my mind prep for Monday.
> One: the phone charges in the kitchen now, not by my bed. *(plugging it in)*
> Two: I'm reading 10 pages a day. First book: [book title]. *(show cover)*
> Three: 20 minutes of study every day, and this time it's sales training for my new career.
> Four: a notebook. One line every night: what went well today.
>
> **[To camera]**
> I used to read all the time before the kids. I want that woman back.
>
> What's the last book you actually finished? Give me recommendations 👇

**On-screen text:** "Prepping my mind for a 90-day reset (Day 0)"

## 💼 4. Money prep: "I changed career at [4_]" (45–60s)

**Setting:** desk/laptop. Be honest about where you're at; don't overstate it.

> **[Hook]**
> At [4_] I changed career, and most people don't even know what my new job is.
>
> **[Explain simply]**
> It's high-ticket sales. Companies sell expensive things (coaching, courses, services) and they need people to talk to customers on calls. You work remotely, and you earn commission on what you close.
>
> **[Where you're at, honestly]**
> Where I am right now: [e.g. "I've done my training and I'm starting calls this week" / "I've just landed my first role" / "I'm still applying"].
>
> **[Prep]**
> For these 90 days my money goal is [e.g. "my first £X month" / "consistent income I can earn from anywhere"]. I've blocked out [hours] a day for calls and practice, and every week I'll show you my real numbers. Even when they're rubbish.
>
> **[To camera]**
> If you've ever thought "it's too late for me to start something new," stick around.

**On-screen text:** "New career at [4_]. Here's my money plan for the next 90 days"
**Rules:** real numbers only. When you share earnings, add "not typical, results vary" in the caption.

## ✈️ 5. Place prep: "Where should I move?" (40–60s)

**Setting:** map, laptop with flights or places, or a suitcase.

> **[Hook]**
> By the end of these 90 days I want to be living abroad. And I haven't decided where yet. 🙈
>
> **[Shortlist]**
> Here's my shortlist: [Country/city 1], [2] and [3].
> What I need: [sunshine, a lower cost of living, good wifi for my calls, an airport near home for the kids].
>
> **[Honest bit]**
> The scary part isn't the move. It's telling the kids. *(laugh)* That'll be its own video.
>
> **[Ask]**
> Help me choose. Comment 1, 2 or 3, or tell me somewhere I haven't thought of. 👇

**On-screen text:** "Moving abroad at [4_] after my kids left. Help me choose 👇"
**Why it works:** asking people to vote in the comments is one of the best ways to boost a new account.

## Weekend filming checklist
- [ ] **Light:** face a window; avoid overhead lights. A £20 ring light helps for evening diaries
- [ ] **Sound:** film close to the phone or use a £15 clip-on mic. Sound matters more than video quality
- [ ] **Batch it:** film all 5 on Saturday, edit in CapCut, and schedule them
- [ ] **Captions:** CapCut auto-captions, big and centred (most people watch on mute)
- [ ] **Cover image:** the same style for every video ("Day X/90" in the same font and colour) so your grid looks like a series
- [ ] **Day 0 private record:** photos, measurements, screen time, bank or income snapshot, energy/mood score. You'll use these on Day 90
- [ ] **Hold the guide link for now:** keep the "Comment AGAIN" CTA for Day 1 onwards (Week 1 of the guide needs writing first)
