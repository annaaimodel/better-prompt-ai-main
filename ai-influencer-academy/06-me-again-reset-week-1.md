## Hello, you 💛

If you're reading this, maybe your house has gone quiet too.

For twenty-odd years my days were built around everyone else: school runs, packed lunches, lifts, worry. Then the kids grew up and left, and I looked around and thought: *who am I now?*

So for 90 days I'm rebuilding five things, and filming every day so we can compare **now vs then**:

| | Pillar | My daily non-negotiables |
|---|---|---|
| 🥗 | **Health** | High protein at every meal · fewer carbs · **2 litres of water** |
| 💪 | **Fitness** | **One workout every day.** Any workout. A walk counts |
| 💷 | **Finances** | **No credit card.** Plus one thing a day to increase my income |
| 💼 | **Career** | Working towards a new role. For me, **securing a new closing offer** in high-ticket sales |
| 📚 | **Knowledge** | **10 pages a day** + practising the skills for my work |

**This guide is exactly what I'm doing.** Each week you'll get the same five daily habits, plus one **weekly focus** with tips to make them easier.

Your career and knowledge goals might look different from mine: a new job, a promotion, going back to study, starting a side hustle. That's perfect. Make them yours.

> ⚠️ *I'm sharing what I'm doing, not giving medical or financial advice. If you have health conditions, are on medication, or haven't exercised in a while, check with your GP before changing your diet or starting daily workouts.*

## Week 1 set-up checklist
- [ ] A **2L water bottle** (draw times on it: 10am, 12pm, 2pm, 4pm, 6pm 😂)
- [ ] High-protein basics in the kitchen: eggs, Greek yoghurt, chicken, tinned tuna, cottage cheese, lentils, protein shakes if you like them
- [ ] Carb swaps ready. Reduce, don't ban: more veg on the plate, a smaller portion of rice/pasta/bread
- [ ] Workout plan for the week written down (see ideas below)
- [ ] Credit card away ✅
- [ ] Book by the bed
- [ ] Tracker printed or saved (below)

### Workout ideas (pick one a day, 20+ minutes)
- Brisk walk or hike
- A free YouTube home workout (search "beginner low impact workout 20 min")
- Swimming, a class, cycling, dancing round the kitchen
- Rest-ish days: gentle stretching or yoga still counts. **The habit is the win this week**

### High-protein day example (what mine looks like)
- **Breakfast:** eggs + spinach, or Greek yoghurt + berries
- **Lunch:** big salad with chicken or tuna
- **Snack:** cottage cheese, a protein shake, or a boiled egg
- **Dinner:** salmon/chicken/tofu + loads of veg + a small portion of potatoes or rice
