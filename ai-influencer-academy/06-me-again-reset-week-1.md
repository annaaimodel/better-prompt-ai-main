# The Me Again Reset
### Week 1: *Start small. Start now.*
*The free 90-day plan I'm following, one week at a time. New week every Sunday.*

## Hello, you 💛

If you're reading this, maybe your house has gone quiet too.

For twenty-odd years my days were built around everyone else: school runs, packed lunches, lifts, worry. Then the kids grew up and left, and I looked around and thought: *who am I now?*

So I'm doing 90 days to find out. I'm rebuilding four things:

| | Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 🥗 | **Body** | Moving every day, eating like I like myself, sleeping properly |
| 📚 | **Mind** | Reading, learning, less scrolling |
| 💼 | **Money** | A new income I'm proud of (for me, a new career in high-ticket sales) |
| ✈️ | **Place** | Building a life I actually want to wake up in (for me, abroad) |

**This guide is exactly what I'm doing.** Every week there's **one small habit per pillar**. That's four things, none of them huge. We're not overhauling our lives on day one. We're laying bricks.

Your "Money" and "Place" might look different from mine. A new job, a side hustle, going back to study, redoing your home, a big trip, moving nearer the sea. That's perfect. Make it yours.

> ⚠️ *I'm sharing what I'm doing, not giving medical or financial advice. If you have health conditions or haven't exercised in a while, check with your GP before you start.*

## Week 1 habits

### 🥗 Body: a 20-minute walk, every day
That's it. No diet, no gym, no 5am bootcamp.
- Any pace. Round the block twice counts.
- Put your trainers by the door tonight.
- **Bonus (optional):** have one glass of water before your morning tea or coffee.

*Why:* walking is the easiest habit to keep, and keeping a promise to yourself every day is the real point of Week 1.

### 📚 Mind: 10 pages a day + the phone sleeps outside your bedroom
- Any book you fancy. Fiction counts. Fun counts.
- Charge your phone in the kitchen. Use a £5 alarm clock if you need one.

*Why:* the last thing you see at night and the first thing you see in the morning shape your whole day. Make them yours.

### 💼 Money: write your "why"
Once this week, sit down with a cuppa and answer:
1. What do I want my income to give me? (Freedom? Security? Travel? Pride?)
2. What would I do for work if I wasn't scared?
3. What's one small step towards it I could take this week? (Research a course, update your CV, message someone who does that job.)

*Why:* on hard days your "why" is what gets you to do the work.

### ✈️ Place: describe your dream ordinary day
Not a holiday, an ordinary Tuesday. Write it like a diary entry:
- Where do you wake up? What can you see out of the window?
- What do you do in the morning? Who do you talk to?
- What does the evening feel like?

*Why:* you can't build a life you haven't pictured. This becomes your north star for the next 90 days.
