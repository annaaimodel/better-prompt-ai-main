## PART A — ManyChat flow

**Trigger:** a comment containing `AGAIN` (any case, e.g. "again", "Again!", "AGAIN 💛") on **any post or Reel**.
**Instagram:** full flow below. **TikTok:** use ManyChat's TikTok integration if it's available in your region. If not, put the guide sign-up page in your TikTok bio and say "link in bio" in TikTok captions.

### Step 1 — Public comment reply (rotate)
- "Sent you a DM 💛"
- "It's in your DMs! Check your message requests if you can't see it"
- "On its way, so glad you're joining me 💛"
- "Sent! Day 1 together 🙌"

### Step 2 — Opening DM (the button keeps the conversation open)
> Hello lovely 💛 thank you for commenting!
>
> I'll send you **The Me Again Reset**: the exact 90-day plan I'm following, one week at a time. Week 1 is ready now.
>
> Tap below and I'll send it over 👇
>
> **[ Yes please! ]**

### Step 3 — Email capture
> Where shall I send it? Pop your email below 📩
>
> You'll get Week 1 straight away, then a new week every Sunday, plus my honest weekly update (the good, the bad and the real numbers). Unsubscribe anytime.

*ManyChat: "Email" user input → sync to Beehiiv/Kit with tag `me-again`.*

### Step 4 — Delivery + a question
> Done ✅ Check your inbox (and promotions/spam just in case).
>
> Can I ask what's brought you here? 👇
>
> **[ Empty nest ]** **[ Want a career change ]** **[ Health reset ]** **[ Dreaming of moving ]**

*Tag each answer (`why-emptynest`, `why-career`, `why-health`, `why-move`). This tells you what your audience cares about most, and shapes the paid challenge.*

### Step 5 — 24h nudge (only if they didn't open or click)
> Hey! Did Week 1 arrive OK? Here's the direct link in case: **[link]** 💛 Your first job is just a 20-minute walk.

### Fallback (no email given)
> No worries! You can grab it here 👉 **[sign-up page link]**

## PART B — Emails

### Email 1 — Immediately: delivers Week 1
**Subject:** Week 1 is here 💛
**Preview:** Four small things. That's all.

> Hi {first_name | lovely},
>
> I'm so glad you're here.
>
> Here's Week 1 of The Me Again Reset 👉 **[Download Week 1]**
>
> It's the exact plan I'm following. This week is gentle:
> 🥗 a 20-minute walk every day
> 📚 10 pages a day + your phone sleeps outside the bedroom
> 💼 write down *why* you want a new income
> ✈️ describe your dream ordinary day
>
> Before you start, fill in the **Day 0 snapshot** on page 2. You'll thank me on Day 90.
>
> A new week lands every Sunday. I'll be filming every day on [@handle] if you want to keep each other going.
>
> See you on the walk,
> Anna
>
> *P.S. Hit reply and tell me one thing you want to be different in 90 days. I read every reply.*

### Email 2 — Day 3: the honest one
**Subject:** The quiet bit nobody talks about
**Preview:** (and why I'm doing this publicly)

> Hi {first_name | lovely},
>
> Can I be honest? The hardest part of the kids leaving wasn't the goodbye. It was the Tuesday afternoon two weeks later, when the house was silent and I realised I didn't know what I *liked* anymore.
>
> That's why I'm doing this in public. Not because I've got it figured out, but because I don't, and I've got a feeling you might not either.
>
> So, how's the walking going? Even if you've only managed one day, that's one more than last week. The rule is just: **never miss twice.**
>
> Anna

### Email 3 — Sunday (Day 7): Week 2 + weekly update
**Subject:** Week 1 done ✅ (here's my real scorecard)
**Preview:** + Week 2 is ready

> Hi {first_name | lovely},
>
> One week down! Here's my honest scorecard:
> - Walks: [__/7]
> - Pages read: [__]
> - Phone out of bedroom: [__/7]
> - Energy: [__/10] (it was [__] on Day 0)
> - Money: [one honest line about the week's progress]
> - Place: [one line, e.g. "Shortlist down to 3, you voted for __"]
>
> **Week 2 is here** 👉 **[Download Week 2]**
>
> How did you get on? Reply with your score out of 7. No judgement, I promise.
>
> Anna

**From then on:** every Sunday = the next week's guide + your real scorecard. Around Day 60, start mentioning the paid challenge waitlist ("Reply IN if you want to do the next 90 days with me in a small group").
