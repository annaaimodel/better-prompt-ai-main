# My 90-Day Plan: Now vs Then
*The 5 pillars, the daily non-negotiables, and exactly what to record so Day 1 vs Day 90 is a real comparison.*

**Day 1:** Monday 12 October 2026 · **Day 30:** Tue 10 Nov · **Day 60:** Thu 10 Dec · **Day 90:** Sat 9 Jan 2027

---

## The 5 pillars

| | Pillar | Daily non-negotiables | 90-day goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥗 | **Health** | High protein at every meal · reduced carbs · **2L water** | Feel and look healthier, with more energy |
| 💪 | **Fitness** | **1 workout every day** (any type, it counts) | Fitter and stronger, measurably |
| 💷 | **Finances** | **No credit card use** · one income-increasing action | Zero new card spending; income up |
| 💼 | **Career** | Applications / outreach / calls until it's secured | **Secure a new closing offer** |
| 📚 | **Knowledge** | **10 pages** + daily closing-skills practice | A better closer, with books finished |

> *Not medical or financial advice. I'm sharing what I'm doing. Check with your GP before big diet or exercise changes.*

## Day 0: the "NOW" record (do this Sunday or Monday morning)

Record everything privately. You choose what to share and when. The more you capture now, the better the Day 90 reveal.

### 🥗 Health
- [ ] **Photos:** front, side, back. **Same spot, same light, same outfit, same time of day** (morning). Repeat on Days 30/60/90
- [ ] **Measurements:** waist, hips, chest, upper arm, thigh (weight optional)
- [ ] **A normal day of eating:** photograph everything you eat on a typical day (this is your "before plate")
- [ ] **Water now:** roughly how much you drink a day: ___
- [ ] **Energy / sleep / mood (1–10):** ___ / ___ / ___
- [ ] **One clothing item** that's tight now. Try it on at each checkpoint (great visual content)
