# AURUM — community launch posts

Ready-to-copy posts for seeding the board in the communities you've already
catalogued in `/directory`. Replace **`[LINK]`** with your live URL
(currently `https://better-prompt-ai-ashy.vercel.app/`).

> **Strongly recommended first:** put a **custom domain** on it (e.g. `aurum.jobs`
> / `aurumclosers.com`). A `vercel.app` link looks unfinished, converts worse, and
> several Facebook groups auto-flag `vercel.app` URLs as spam. A clean domain is
> the single cheapest credibility upgrade you can make before promoting.

## Rules of the road (so you don't get banned)
- **Read each group's rules first.** Many ban links in posts — if so, put the value
  in the post and the link in the **first comment**, or DM on request.
- **Lead with usefulness, never "check out my site."** You're sharing a free tool.
- **Don't blast all channels at once.** 2–3 posts/week, rotate, and actually reply
  to comments — engagement is what the algorithms reward.
- **Be a real person.** Answer the "where do I find legit remote closing roles?"
  threads that already exist — that's warmer than a cold post.

## After you post
- **Capture the traffic:** make sure the homepage email signup is visible, then
  send a weekly "new roles this week" email so first-time visitors come back.
- **Track what works:** the visitor digest (`jobs/analytics.md`) refreshes daily —
  watch which days/channels move the **Last 24 hours** number, and double down.
