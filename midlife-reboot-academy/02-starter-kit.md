# The AI Influencer Starter Kit
*Build your first AI creator this weekend, the right way.*

> **How to use this kit:** work through it in order. By the end you'll have a niche, a character, your first 10 post ideas, and a way to get paid. Set aside one afternoon.

---

## 1. Pick a niche that pays

You need a niche **people buy from** and where an AI character is **honest**: it doesn't depend on claiming personal results.

### The 5-point niche test
Score each from 0 to 2. **7 or more = go.**
| Question | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can they earn from products in it (affiliate or TikTok Shop)? | No | Some | Loads |
| Can an AI character do it **without faking results**? | No | Partly | Fully |
| Can you make 30+ posts without running dry? | No | Maybe | Easily |
| Is it visual (looks good in a 3-second scroll)? | No | Sort of | Very |
| Can you name the exact person it's for? | No | Vaguely | Precisely |

### 15 niches that pass
| Niche | Example angle | Main income |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet luxury fashion | "Looks £500, cost £60" | Affiliate / TikTok Shop |
| Petite fashion | "Outfits that don't drown you" | Affiliate |
| Modest fashion | Styling for work and events | Affiliate + digital guide |
| Men's smart-casual | "Dress better in 10 pieces" | Affiliate |
| Cosy home & interiors | Small-flat styling on a budget | Amazon Storefront |
| Desk setups | Aesthetic workspace builds | Affiliate (tech) |
| Travel outfits | "What I'd pack for Lisbon" | Affiliate + travel guides |
| Capsule wardrobes | One wardrobe, 30 outfits | Digital product |
| Seasonal trends | "Autumn's 5 key pieces" | TikTok Shop |
| Kitchen & hosting | Table styling, gadgets | Amazon |
| Stationery & planners | Plan-with-me | Affiliate + printables |
| Book aesthetics | Cosy reading nooks, book picks | Affiliate |
| Pet character | A cat with opinions | Pet product affiliate |
| Gift guides | "Gifts under £30 for…" | Affiliate (peaks seasonally) |
| Wedding guest outfits | Outfits by dress code | Affiliate |

### ❌ Avoid
- **Fitness/weight-loss "transformations"**: an AI body can't honestly show results.
- **Skincare "it cleared my skin"**: the same problem.
- **Finance/trading tips**: regulated, and a trust disaster.
- **Adult content**: payment processors, ad bans and your reputation.
- **Anything that imitates a real person.**

## 2. Build your character

### Character brief template
Fill this in, then paste it into your image tool.
```
NAME:            [first name only, easy to say]
AGE / VIBE:      [e.g. late 20s, polished but approachable]
LOCATION FEEL:   [city/setting that matches the niche]
LOOK:            [hair, eyes, 1–2 distinctive features]
PALETTE:         [3–5 colours they're always in]
PERSONALITY:     [3 adjectives + how they talk]
CATCHPHRASE:     [the line people will repeat]
WHO THEY HELP:   [exact person: "women 25–35 who want to look expensive on a budget"]
```

### Worked example: Margot
```
NAME:            Margot
AGE / VIBE:      Late 20s, polished but approachable
LOCATION FEEL:   London — cafés, Marylebone streets, a light-filled flat
LOOK:            Long chestnut waves, hazel eyes, light freckles
PALETTE:         Camel, cream, black, navy, white + gold jewellery
PERSONALITY:     Warm, dry, practical — talks like a friend who works in fashion
CATCHPHRASE:     "Looks £500. Cost £60."
WHO SHE HELPS:   Women 25–35 who want to look expensive on a high-street budget
```

### 6 rules for a consistent face
1. **Make a character sheet first**: one reference with the front, side and full body. Every image after that uses it.
2. **Lock the hair, eyes and one feature** (e.g. freckles) and never change them.
3. **Keep the same palette.** It makes the character recognisable before people even see the face.
4. **Use 3–4 recurring locations.** Repeated settings look like a real life.
5. **Save the seed and settings** of your best images and reuse them.
6. **Reject anything off**: wrong eye colour, extra fingers, a different face shape. One bad image breaks the illusion.

## 3. Prompt pack (10 prompts to adapt)

Swap the **[brackets]** for your character's details.

1. **Outfit reveal**: *[Character], full-body mirror selfie in a bright minimalist flat, wearing [outfit], natural daylight, phone partially covering face, realistic, 9:16*
2. **Street style**: *[Character] walking on a [city] street, candid mid-stride, wearing [outfit], shallow depth of field, golden hour, editorial photo*
3. **Café moment**: *[Character] sitting at a café window with a coffee, [outfit], soft morning light, relaxed smile, lifestyle photo*
4. **Flat lay**: *Overhead flat lay of [5 items] on cream linen, [palette] tones, soft shadows, product photography*
5. **Detail shot**: *Close-up of [character]'s hands holding [bag/item], gold jewellery, neutral background, shallow focus*
6. **1 piece, 5 ways**: *A grid of 5 photos of [character] styling [item] five different ways, consistent lighting, [palette]*
7. **GRWM**: *[Character] getting ready at a vanity, natural makeup, morning light, candid, vertical frame*
8. **Seasonal**: *[Character] in [season] setting (autumn leaves / snowy street / beach promenade), wearing [outfit], cinematic*
9. **Dupe check**: *Split image, left: [designer-style item] on [character], right: [high-street version], same pose, same lighting*
10. **Behind the scenes**: *[Character] laughing, slightly blurred, as if caught between photos, warm tones, film grain*

**Turning images into video:** animate 3–5 second clips (a walk, a turn, a hair flick). Add text on screen, trending audio and a voiceover. Make it a 15–25 second Reel.

## 5. Get paid: the starter setup

Set these up **this week**, even before you have followers:
- [ ] **Amazon Associates / Amazon Influencer Storefront**: build a "Shop my looks" page
- [ ] **LTK or ShopMy**: fashion and home affiliate links (apply once you have some content)
- [ ] **Link in bio** (Beacons, Linktree or Stan): one page for every link
- [ ] **TikTok Shop affiliate**: unlocks at a follower threshold (check the current one in your region)
- [ ] **One simple digital product** later on (a style guide, checklist or capsule plan), £9–19

**Rule:** only link products that genuinely fit the content you made. Relevant links convert, random ones don't.

---

## 6. Play it straight (this protects your account)

- ✅ Put **"AI creator"** in your bio.
- ✅ Turn on each platform's **AI-generated label** for realistic AI images and video.
- ✅ Use **#ad / affiliate disclosures** on posts with commission links.
- ❌ Don't claim personal experiences your character can't have ("I lost 10kg", "this cured my acne").
- ❌ Don't use real people's faces, names or brands as if they endorse you.

Being transparent isn't a weakness. "She's AI, and she's still got better taste than me" is a hook people love.
