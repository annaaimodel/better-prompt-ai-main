# Comment-to-DM flow + welcome emails
*Turns "Comment MARGOT" into email subscribers and then into buyers.*

---

## PART A — ManyChat flow

**Trigger:** a comment containing `MARGOT` (also catch `margot`, `Margot`, `MARGOT!`) on **any post or Reel**.
**Platforms:** Instagram first (best support). Add TikTok if ManyChat's TikTok integration is available in your region. Otherwise put the starter kit link in your TikTok bio.

### Step 1 — Public comment reply (rotate these so it doesn't look spammy)
- "Sent! Check your DMs 💌"
- "Just sent it over, check your requests folder if you can't see it 👀"
- "On its way 🙌 Margot says hi"
- "Done! It's in your DMs ✨"

### Step 2 — Opening DM (with a button, so Instagram lets you keep messaging them)
> Hey! 👋 Thanks for commenting. Here's the **AI Influencer Starter Kit**: the niche list, the character template and the exact prompts I'm using for Margot.
>
> Tap below and I'll send it straight over 👇
>
> **[ Send me the kit ]**

### Step 3 — Email capture
> Amazing! Where should I send it? Pop your email below and the kit lands in your inbox in about a minute 📩
>
> *(I'll also send you Margot's weekly numbers: real followers, real £. You can unsubscribe whenever.)*

*ManyChat setup: use an "Email" user input → sync to Beehiiv/Kit with the tag `starter-kit`.*

### Step 4 — Delivery + a question
> Done ✅ Check your inbox (and promotions/spam, just in case).
>
> Quick one so I can send you the right stuff: **where are you at right now?**
>
> **[ Haven't started ]** **[ Made a character ]** **[ Already posting ]**

*Tag each answer (`stage-0`, `stage-1`, `stage-2`). This tells you who's ready for the high-ticket offer later.*

### Step 5 — 24h follow-up (only if they didn't click the email link)
> Hey, did the kit come through OK? If you couldn't find it, here's the direct link: **[link]** 💌

### Fallback (they didn't type an email)
> No worries! You can grab it here instead 👉 **[landing page link]**

## PART B — Welcome email sequence (3 emails)

### Email 1 — Sent immediately
**Subject:** Your AI Influencer Starter Kit 🎁
**Preview:** + the one rule most people get wrong

> Hey {first_name | there},
>
> Here it is: **[Download the Starter Kit]**
>
> Inside: the niche test, 15 niches that pay, the character template and 10 prompts.
>
> One thing before you dive in. **The #1 mistake I see is picking a niche where an AI character has to fake results**: fitness transformations, "this cleared my skin". It gets accounts flagged and kills trust. Section 1 of the kit shows you how to avoid it.
>
> Every week I'll send you Margot's real numbers: followers, views, and every £ she earns. The good, the bad and the flops.
>
> Talk soon,
> Anna
>
> *P.S. Hit reply and tell me which niche you're thinking about. I read every one.*

### Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** How Margot got her face
**Preview:** (and why I threw away the first 20 versions)

> Hey {first_name | there},
>
> Consistency is the whole game with AI influencers. If her face changes between posts, people scroll past and the algorithm notices.
>
> Here's what I do:
> 1. **One character sheet first** (front, side, full body), and every image is built from it
> 2. **Locked features**: chestnut waves, hazel eyes, freckles. Never changed.
> 3. **Locked palette**: camel, cream, black, navy. You recognise her before you see her face.
> 4. **3 recurring locations**: her flat, a café, one street. That makes it look like a real life.
>
> I threw away about 20 versions before I had one I could build on. That's normal.
>
> **Your task today:** fill in the character template from the kit (page 2). It takes 10 minutes.
>
> Anna

### Email 3 — Day 4
**Subject:** How AI influencers actually get paid
**Preview:** It's not brand deals (yet)

> Hey {first_name | there},
>
> Everyone thinks AI influencers make money from brand deals. Some do, eventually. But the fastest money is much simpler:
>
> 1. **Affiliate links**: Amazon Storefront, LTK. Live from day one.
> 2. **TikTok Shop**: tag products in your videos and earn commission per sale.
> 3. **A small digital product**: a £9–19 guide that fits your niche.
> 4. **Brand deals**: once you've got numbers to show.
>
> Margot's goal is £1,000 in her first 90 days, mostly from #1 and #2. I'm posting every step on my page.
>
> **Coming soon:** I'm opening a small founding group where I'll walk people through building their own, step by step, with me checking their accounts. Founding members get the lowest price it'll ever be.
>
> 👉 **Reply "IN"** and you'll be first to hear when it opens.
>
> Anna
