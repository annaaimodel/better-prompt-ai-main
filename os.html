Your method

Built around your method — not ours

The OS doesn’t ship with a methodology, scripts or an offer baked in. Every installation is built to match one person or one team — and every whisper, script and review is then judged against your way of selling, so the machine keeps you on-method even at minute forty-five.

Your methodology

The framework and rules your calls run on. Any system, any school of selling.

Your scripts

Upload your own, or let the script engine build new ones from your playbook.

Your offer

Price, deliverables, guarantees. The AI never invents a fact you didn’t load.

Your voice

Formality, phrasing, sign-offs. It writes and whispers like you — not like AI.

Your knowledge

Products, objections, Q&A — plus anything you pull in on demand along the way.

Nothing is hard-coded. Swap the offer, change the method, rebuild the scripts — the whole OS re-tunes to whoever it’s built for.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What do I need to run it?
A browser. That’s it. The OS runs on Vercel and works on desktop, laptop and mobile. For Headset mode (hearing both sides of a call) you add your own Deepgram key and share the call tab; mic mode needs nothing extra.
Is it tied to one offer, script or method?
No. Nothing comes pre-loaded: the playbook, methodology, scripts and knowledge cards are all built around the person or team using it — and can be swapped at any time. Any high-ticket offer, any methodology.
Where does my data live?
In your browser (localStorage) — private and fast. You can export/import JSON, restore automatic backups, and optionally turn on cross-device sync, which keeps a private cloud copy keyed to your access code. The AI engines store nothing.
Does the AI listen to my calls?
Only while you’re on the Live tab, only for you, and nothing is retained by the AI engines. Transcripts are saved to the lead in your browser, for your call reviews. You’re responsible for following the call-recording rules that apply where you sell.
How does team access work?
One access list, comma-separated. Each person gets their own code and their own private data. The admin code unlocks the full OS; everyone else runs Lite mode — a focused call-only view they can’t switch off.
Which AI does it use?
The engines run on leading frontier AI models, and we keep them on the best model for each job as the technology evolves — whispers, call review, drafting, objection handling, scripting, coaching, content, parsing, med lookup, mask read and sync.
