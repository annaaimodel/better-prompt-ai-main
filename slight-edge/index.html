The Slight Edge
is built one day at a time

Pick one real goal. We turn it into simple daily actions — easy to do, easy not to do — with measurable mini-targets you watch compound over weeks. Then you just show up.

The method

The seven slight-edge habits

Show up. Most people never start. You will.
Show up consistently. Consistency beats intensity, every time.
Show up with a positive outlook. Attitude is a daily choice.
Be committed for the long haul. Plant, cultivate, then harvest.
Cultivate a burning desire backed by faith. Want it — and believe it's possible.
Be willing to pay the price. Do what others won't.
Practise slight-edge integrity. Do it even when no one is watching.

Habits as set out in Jeff Olson's The Slight Edge.
